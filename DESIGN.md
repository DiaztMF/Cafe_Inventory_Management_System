# Design System: Lunar Coffee Landing Page
**Project ID:** 6502338211096194210

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
The atmosphere of Lunar Coffee is defined by calmness, warmth, and simplicity. The visual experience is crafted to feel as relaxing and serene as enjoying a quiet morning coffee. The design leans into a clean, minimalist aesthetic that relies heavily on ample whitespace to create a sense of breathing room ("rasa lega"). It's a soft, approachable, and highly inviting environment.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
*   **Deep Roast / Primary** (`#3E2723`): A rich, deep brown used for primary actions, heavy contrast elements, and establishing the core coffee-centric branding.
*   **Warm Latte / Secondary Container** (`#D7CCC8`): A soft, muted beige used for secondary backgrounds, subtle highlights, and softening the overall contrast.
*   **Fresh Mint / Accent** (`#B2DFDB`): A refreshing, cool accent color used sparingly to draw attention or provide a gentle contrast to the warm, earthy tones.
*   **Creamy White / Base Background** (`#FAFAFA`): An airy, off-white foundation that keeps the interface clean and prevents the starkness of pure white.
*   **Dark Espresso / Primary Text** (`#212121`): Used for primary typography, offering high legibility while remaining softer than pure black to maintain the calm aesthetic.

## 3. Typography Rules
The typography pairing balances classic elegance with modern readability.
*   **Headlines (Noto Serif):** Used for all major titles and section headers. It provides an elegant, classic, and slightly formal feel. Weights range from Semi-Bold (600) to Bold (700) for strong visual hierarchy.
*   **Body & Labels (Be Vietnam Pro):** A clean, modern sans-serif used for all functional text and descriptions. It ensures legibility. Labels often utilize a heavier weight (600) with added letter-spacing (0.05em) for clarity and a sophisticated touch.

## 4. Component Stylings
*   **Buttons:** Gently rounded shapes (typically `0.5rem` or `8px` radius). Primary buttons utilize the "Deep Roast" color for solid grounding, inviting clicks without aggressive sharp edges.
*   **Cards/Containers:** Feature subtly rounded corners (`0.5rem` to `1rem`) to reinforce the soft, welcoming theme. Backgrounds leverage "Warm Latte" or "Creamy White" tones to subtly separate from the main background. Shadows are minimal to maintain a flat, clean look.
*   **Inputs/Forms:** Clean and minimalist, matching the soft corner radius of buttons and cards, focusing on usability with high-contrast text ("Dark Espresso") against soft backgrounds.

## 5. Layout Principles
*   **Whitespace:** The design heavily prioritizes negative space. Generous margins (`2rem`), wide gutters (`1.5rem`), and large spacing tokens (up to `8rem`) are used to separate sections, preventing clutter and ensuring a relaxed, "unhurried" browsing experience.
*   **Alignment:** Elements are well-structured but given enough room to breathe, avoiding dense packing of information and maintaining a calming flow down the page.
